# RHCSA Mock Exam Questions & Complete Solutions

## My RHCSA Preparation Story

I am a sophomore majoring in Software Engineering at Hainan University.

During my RHCSA preparation, I realized that many students struggle with the practical nature of the exam and often do not know where to start. For me, repeatedly practicing and reviewing mock exam questions turned out to be one of the most effective ways to prepare.

By thoroughly studying and practicing these mock exams, I eventually passed the RHCSA exam with a score of **285 / 300**.

This repository contains the mock exam questions provided in my course, together with complete step-by-step solutions and review notes. I am open-sourcing these materials for free in the hope that they can help other RHCSA candidates prepare more efficiently, especially during the final review stage before the exam. 

## Repository Contents

1. `mock-questions/`

The real RHCSA exam consists of two virtual machines: Node 1 and Node 2. I split the exam tasks into two separate files, with questions matched to the operations required on each node respectively.

2. `solutions/`

Detailed operation steps, command explanations and key reminders for every question.

3. `review-notes/`

Review notes corresponding to the ten RHCSA lectures in my course. These notes summarize the major concepts, commands and lab exercises covered in each class, and can be used as a structured learning path for RHCSA preparation.

## Important Tips

1. RHCSA is a performance-based practical examination. Understanding concepts and practicing operations in a virtual machine environment are both essential.
2. All materials here are for personal study only. Commercial resale or paid distribution is strictly prohibited.
3. If you find any mistakes or have suggestions for improvement, feel free to open an Issue or submit a Pull Request.
4. You need to know how to find commonly used documentation, and after completing each question, you must master how to verify correctness by executing commands and observing output. Finally, I wish all of you high scores and smooth passes on your RHCSA certification exams!
